What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • Your first name, only if you choose to give it.
  • The date and time you signed up and when you confirmed, plus the wording of the consent box you ticked. We keep this as proof that you agreed.

Why we collect it

To send you one email when Kitform launches. We will not email you about anything else unless the sign up box says so. You agree by ticking the box on the signup form.

Who has your information

Your email is stored by MailerLite, the email service we use for the waitlist. It may be stored and processed outside Canada. Only Aaron can open the list. We do not sell your email or share it with anyone else.

The signup form is a page run by MailerLite. When you open it, MailerLite receives information such as your IP address and may use cookies. See MailerLite's own privacy policy on its website for details.

Cookies and trackers on this site

The Kitform pages on kitform.ca do not use analytics, ad trackers, tracking pixels or cookies, and fonts are not loaded from other sites.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If Kitform has not launched within 12 months of your sign up, we will delete the list.

Your choices

  • Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. We act on it without delay and within 10 business days at the latest.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Write to hello@kitform.ca to see, correct or delete your information. We aim to reply within 3 business days.
