Learn CAD one small build at a time.

Kitform is a library of step by step SolidWorks build guides. Each set is laid out like a building set booklet: one change per step, picture first, few words. Pick a level, follow along, and finish a real model.

  • Beginner to advanced
  • Drawings to read
  • Exam practice

How it works

No long videos to pause and rewind. Every set is a short booklet you follow one step at a time.

  1. 1

    Pick a set

    Browse the catalog and choose a project at your level. Each one shows what you will build and roughly how long it takes.

  2. 2

    Follow the booklet

    One change per step, with a clear picture of what the model should look like when you finish the step.

  3. 3

    Finish a real model

    End with a complete part or assembly, plus a drawing you can read and a line you can use on your resume.

Three levels

Start where you are and move up when you are ready.

  • Beginner

    Your first sketches, extrudes and simple parts.

  • Intermediate

    Multi feature parts and small assemblies.

  • Advanced

    Mechanisms, mates and drawings with more detail.

What is in every set

Everything in one place, so you do not have to piece it together from search results.

  • Step by step bookletShort steps, pictures first.
  • Drawings you can readLearn how a finished part is described on paper.
  • Exam practiceOriginal problems for the certification exams.
  • A resume lineA clear way to describe what you built.

Who it is for

Mechanical engineering students and anyone learning SolidWorks who wants to build things, not just watch.
